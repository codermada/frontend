Panther Profile

🐆 Overview

A leopard, especially a black one. Panthers are not a separate species but rather a melanistic variant of leopards or jaguars.

A large American wild cat with a plain tawny to greyish coat, found from Canada to Patagonia; commonly known as a puma or cougar.
