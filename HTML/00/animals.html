Meet the Predators

🐯 Tiger

A very large solitary cat with a yellow-brown coat striped with black, native to the forests of Asia but becoming increasingly rare.

🦁 Lion

A large tawny-coloured cat that lives in prides, found in Africa and north-western India. The male has a flowing shaggy mane and takes little part in hunting, which is done cooperatively by the females.

🐆 Panther

A leopard, especially a black one.

A large American wild cat with a plain tawny to greyish coat, found from Canada to Patagonia; commonly known as a puma.
